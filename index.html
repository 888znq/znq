Live row lines: 60 lines crossing on the real price

Session = one 120 second window, aligned to the clock (every 2 minutes). Cycle 60 = rows 0 to 59 (60 lines). Row r samples the price at seconds r, r+cycle, r+2×cycle … starting at its own second. Each row draws the real 1-second price path from its start second onward, not straight chords between stops. Green segment = price rose since the previous stop, red = fell.
